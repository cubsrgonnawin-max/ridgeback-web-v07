Current capabilities

What Ridgeback Global can deliver today, across engineering practice and compliance systems.

01

Traffic Engineering

Analysis and design for roadway and intersection performance, developed to agency standards and defensible under review.

  • Traffic impact analysis and corridor studies
  • Intersection capacity and operational analysis
  • Signal warrant evaluation
  • Traffic control and work-zone planning
02

Environmental Engineering

Environmental analysis and documentation supporting infrastructure delivery and the approvals it depends on.

  • Environmental review documentation and technical support
  • Environmental permitting strategy and agency coordination
  • Regulatory requirement identification across jurisdictions
03

Structural Engineering

Structural analysis and design for infrastructure elements, sized and detailed to applicable codes and standards.

  • Structural analysis and design calculations
  • Drainage structures, culverts, and retaining structures
  • Code and standard compliance review
04

Stormwater & Grading Design

Preliminary and detailed design for stormwater management and site grading, engineered to local and state standards.

  • Stormwater management design and detention/retention sizing
  • Site grading and drainage design
  • Erosion and sediment control planning
  • Hydrologic and hydraulic calculation packages
05

Permitting & Regulatory Navigation

Determining which permits apply — local, state, and federal — and in what sequence, so approvals don't stall a project that is otherwise ready to proceed.

  • Jurisdictional permit identification and sequencing strategy
  • Agency coordination and submittal packages
  • Review comment response and resubmittal
06

Engineering Reports & Technical Documentation

Scope-centered reports and calculation packages that cite the governing codes and standards, written to be relied on by reviewers rather than merely filed.

  • Calculation packages with traceable methodology
  • Technical memoranda and design narratives
  • Peer-reviewable engineering documentation
07

Agentic AI & Compliance Systems

Development of governed, auditable AI systems for infrastructure delivery — the discipline behind Ridgeback OS.

  • Compliance-first agent architecture and governance design
  • Obligation tracking and evidence-management system design
  • Human-in-the-loop review gate design for automated workflows
